# Academic-performance-monitoring-for-NWU-Students
Designing and Implementing Management Science (Decision  Support System) to Improve Academic Performance Among University Students 
Aim 
This study aims to develop a student-centred, user-friendly system to support and improve 
academic performance monitoring among university students. 
3.4. Objectives 
To achieve this aim, the following objectives will be pursued: 
1. To conduct a review of existing literature on academic performance management 
science, students as a primary user, and mathematical programming models to 
establish a theoretical foundation. 
2. Design efficient translation modes and techniques to make the mathematical models 
computational.  
3. To develop a functional, open-source, and user-friendly digital artefact that 
encapsulates the models and the basic use of the prototype as the foundation. 
4. To evaluate the artefact in terms of usability (accessibility), effectiveness, and its 
potential to support improved academic decision-making among university students.
